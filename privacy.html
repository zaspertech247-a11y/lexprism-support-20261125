LexPrism Privacy

Workspace records

Grammar names, rule text and frozen analyses are stored in the app workspace on your device. LexPrism requires no account and uses no advertising or tracking SDK.

Sharing and clearing records

When you use Share PDF or Share CSV, the destination you choose in the system sheet receives the chosen file. Reports → About → Clear local workspace removes the workspace records and any temporary export copies held by LexPrism. Files saved elsewhere follow your device or service settings.

Contact

When you contact support, the information you send is used to answer your enquiry.
